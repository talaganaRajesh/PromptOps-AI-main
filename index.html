Core Intelligence Modules

Six Proprietary Modules. One Complete PromptOps DNA Platform.

PromptOps AI combines AI Fingerprinting, Prompt Failure Forensics, Intent-to-Workflow Synthesis, Outcome-Learning Memory, Community Logic Network, and Workflow Equity Analytics into one unified browser-based SaaS platform no-code, real-time, and SME-optimised.

Module 01

PromptOps AI Fingerprinting Engine

Captures the hidden logic of high-performing AI interactions and stores them as reusable, benchmarked workflow assets that can be cloned across entire teams.

The proprietary Fingerprinting engine uses machine learning to detect and encode the "behavioural DNA" of successful prompt chains tone, structure, accuracy patterns so expert-level logic is permanently preserved. When a senior professional creates a high-performing workflow, PromptOps AI fingerprints that logic so junior staff can replicate expert-level results instantly, eliminating the "logic loss" of staff turnover.

Prompt Failure Forensics Engine (PFFE)

Automatically identifies root causes of AI "quiet failures" hallucinations, context drift, logic errors by analysing the delta between intended instructions and executed outputs.

The PFFE pinpoints precisely where a workflow breaks down and provides actionable optimisations in real time. UK businesses can scale AI usage without the 84% manual-checking bottleneck that currently stifles productivity. Our pilot study showed an 18% average improvement in hallucination rates across 1,200 tested interactions using forensic calibration.
Module 03

Intent-to-Workflow Auto-Synthesis

Translates unstructured business requests into real-time operational conditions without the need for any manual prompt engineering.

When a user submits a complex task, the platform automatically decomposes it into a structured, multi-step workflow. Performance-Weighted Workflow Evolution then self-optimises workflows that yield better outcomes are automatically prioritised and refined. Logic Loss Mitigation ensures new hires can immediately utilise the established PromptOps logic of their predecessors, cutting onboarding time from 45 days to 14 days.
